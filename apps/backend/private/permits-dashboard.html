Behind the numbers.

This dashboard is a local view of your workbook. It does not issue permits or confirm access with an airport system.

  1. One permit, one count. Records are matched by Permit Number. Where a number repeats, the latest Issue Date is used; ties use the last source row. Earlier records remain available in the details.
  2. Gates are linked, not duplicated. Unique Gate + Permit Number pairs are joined to the permit. The same gate number at different stations is shown separately.
  3. Status comes first. Cancelled permits are excluded from valid and upcoming expiry counts. Other statuses are calculated from the start/end dates at the selected analysis date. Unrecognized or updated-only statuses are marked for review.
  4. Renewal window: 30 days. Expiry today is included. Expired means the end date is before the analysis date. Historical expired permits do not automatically mean a person currently has no access.
  5. People are counted separately. Distinct national IDs identify people; if missing, the exact name is used. No processing lead times or pending renewals are inferred from this workbook.
  6. Station map. Station markers filter this dashboard. An absent station means no records in the supplied workbook, not that the airport has no permits. Counts on the map reflect the other active filters; status tabs filter the register only. Drag to pan and use the wheel or map buttons to zoom. New station codes in the permit data appear automatically in the station list. Locations can come from optional Latitude / Longitude columns, an optional Stations sheet (Station, City, Latitude, Longitude), or the Station locations menu. Missing coordinates are flagged rather than guessed. Location edits in the menu last for this session; export them and include a Stations sheet to reuse them. Boundaries: Natural Earth, via world-atlas (public domain).
  7. Update locally. Import an .xlsx with IndividualPermitReport and Gates. It is read in your browser and is not uploaded. Imported changes last until you reload this HTML. CSV and print reflect your current register filters.
